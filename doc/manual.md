# Manual

This page provides detailed information on how to play the game, its controls and mechanics.
A shorter version is available inside the game.

## Slenderman

In each level, you must fulfill its respective objective while Slenderman is after you.

Slenderman is the main enemy of the game.
He is a being that always knows where you are and can teleport and turn invisible at his will.
Once you start progress in the level's objective (for example, collecting the first page in level 1), Slenderman spawns and begins chasing you.
His aggression level and abilities scale as you progress.

Slenderman gets closer when you are not looking, but looking at him causes damage to your health (represented as static).
If you look at him for too long or you let him get too close, he gets you.
Slenderman can teleport, instantly moving close to you, and sometimes will teleport to right where you are looking at.

At lower aggression levels, Slenderman occasionally turns invisible and becomes idle for a few seconds, but beware, once he comes back he teleports to where you are.

## Ghosts

Ghosts are commonly seen in most levels.
Most ghosts are peaceful, except for a few in level 2 and extra level 1.

### Level 2 ghost

Level 2's ghost is a secondary enemy.
He spawns alongside Slenderman and chases you when you are not looking.
Like Slenderman, he can also teleport, and becomes more aggressive as you release more souls.


If he catches you, he stuns and blinds you for a moment, giving Slenderman leverage to get you.
Eventually he becomes too fast to outrun, and getting rid of him is the only reliable way to survive.
If you shine your lantern onto him, he fades out and disappears for a while. He comes back shortly after.

### Extra level 1 ghosts

Extra level 1 is full of ghosts.
The ghost seen in level 2 is seen here, eventually spawning but less aggressive than on level 2.

There are also other dangerous ghosts which wander around the town.
Their faces resemble the faces seen in jack-o'-lanterns.
These ghosts do not do any harm as long as you don't get too close.
If you get too close, they chase you for a while until you are far enough and enough time has passed.
If they catch you, they stun you similarly to the other ghost.

## Tips

## Distant Slender
If too far, Slenderman cannot harm you for looking at him.
However, doing so makes him teleport more often. 

## Light source
Your light source lasts indefinitely, use it as much as you want.

### Stamina
You have limited stamina, preserve it while it's safe, and use it in your most crucial moments.
To speed up your progress, you can also moderately use stamina.
Your stamina recharges when you are not running, but it recharges even faster if you stand still.

### Audio cues
The ghost seen in level 2 whispers quietly, you eventually hear it if he gets close.
Use this to know when he's nearby.

The other ghosts in extra level 1 emit a bell sound when they spot you and start chasing you.

### Dangerous rooms
Some places might be dangerous to visit, such as the abandoned house in level 2 or smaller rooms in level 3, because they corner you with no way to escape if Slender is near.
Try to visit these places earlier in the game when Slenderman is less aggressive.

### Follow the path
In level 1, 2 and extra 1, all objective elements (pages, souls, pumpkins) are in remarkable places or on the dirt/stone paths.
You don't have to stray far from the paths or walk in the grass aimlessly to find them.
